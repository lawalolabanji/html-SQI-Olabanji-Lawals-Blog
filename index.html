There are numerous schools in Nigeria. The are government owned primary, secondary and tertiary institutions. The cost of attenting the government schools has been increasing over the years. From my observation, the primary and secondary schools are in a state of neglect compared to the universities. The minimum educational qualification required to become a candidate for elected positions in Nigera is the secondary school education (WAEC, GCE). My opinion on this matter is that government owned primary and secondary schools should be upgraded such that there are upgraded curriculum, qualified teachers, good disciplinary team, serene learning environment, relevant sporting activities, good food, transport system for day students, good hostels for boarding students etc.
